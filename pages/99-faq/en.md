# FAQ

## Is everything on the site free now? Will it always be free?

No. This is temporary while the application is in active development. You are taking part in beta testing. When the course and learning materials settle and reach the planned volume, access to the application will become paid.

## I do not like the voice used for the materials. What can I do?

Open settings and choose another voice available in your system. At the moment the materials use your device's built-in Web Speech API. It usually works better on Android, Windows and Ubuntu, and worse on iOS and Mac. Later, when the course materials settle, we plan to record all words and phrases with a professional voice.

## The speech rate is too fast. How can I slow it down?

Open settings through the gear icon in the menu and choose a comfortable speech rate.

## Why do exercises not show transcriptions and translations like the new words section?

This is intentional. We want students to learn words well and recall them quickly. Exercise hints are available only under the material block, and that inconvenience is deliberate. If you are not sure about a word, its transcription or translation, it is better to return to the words before doing the exercises.

## I want to always see Cyrillic transcription in new words. Can I do that?

Yes. Open settings through the gear icon, find "New words", and set the transcription you need to "Always". It will appear next to the word without pressing the eye button.

## Why can't I show the original exercise text next to transcription and translation in exercise hints?

This is a methodological decision. The student should not have convenient access to exercise text with all hints, because that weakens the need to learn the words properly. We do not plan to remove this limitation for now.

## Why do you not have movies and TV series?

We do not chase quantity. Our priority is quality. For cartoons we manually split material into chapters, cut video and subtitles into smaller parts, compress video for fast loading, and prepare subtitles so individual phrases can be played by clicking them. This is realistic for a curated collection of well-known cartoons, but not for thousands of hours of films and series.

## Why are there few materials in Topics?

We add topic collections on demand. If you need a specific topic prepared and published in our format, write to us through the contacts in the application footer. If the topic fits the project, we will prepare it and add it to the application.

## There are already countless English-learning apps. Why make another one?

We think we have something to say in this field. Our "1 rule + 10 words" course method is unusual: we do not use words the student has not learned yet, and we have tooling that checks this and other details.

Peppa Pig is a great language-learning material, but high-quality subtitles are hard to find. We prepare subtitles manually and correct phrase timings. The same applies to BBC 6 Minute English: it is excellent material for language, vocabulary and general knowledge. We approach the site, the materials and the people who use the application with care.

## I plan to launch a language app for a rare language. Can I use your work instead of starting from scratch?

Yes. We sell the template and the knowledge we have accumulated. We can install everything for you and explain how to fill it with your own materials. Write to us through the contacts in the page footer.

## I really like the service. How can I help?

There is a "write to the developer" link in the application footer. Write there and tell us how you would like to help. Feedback, sharing the project with friends, posting in groups, and other help are welcome.

## Is your English British or American?

The course uses BBC materials as a foundation, including phonetics lessons and 6 Minute English, and course transcriptions follow British standards. In cartoons you may hear different varieties, mostly American.
