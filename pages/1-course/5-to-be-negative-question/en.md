# 5. Aren't you tall? Isn't she quiet?

## Negative question patterns {new}

## Answer words {new|interjection}

no

## Adjectives {new|adjective}

tall
short
rich
poor
brave
afraid
quiet
loud
clean
dirty

## Questions

Are you not tall?
Aren't you tall?
No, you're not tall.

Is he not rich?
Isn't he rich?
No, he isn't rich.

Isn't she quiet?
No, she isn't quiet.

Isn't it clean?
No, it isn't clean.

Aren't we brave?
No, we're not brave.

Aren't they poor?
No, they aren't poor.
