# 11. There is a sofa in the room.

## There is / There are {new}

there is
there are
there isn't
there aren't

## Nouns {new|noun}

house
kitchen
bedroom
bathroom
sofa
bed
mirror
carpet

## The patterns {new}

there is a sofa in the room
there are beds in the bedroom
there is a mirror near the door
there are carpets on the floor

## Difference

There is a book on the table.
A book is on the table.

There are books on the shelf.
The books are on the shelf.

There is a sofa in the room.
The sofa is in the room.
