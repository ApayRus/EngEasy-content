# 8. These are books. Those are boxes.

## Demonstratives {new|demonstrative}

these
those

## Nouns {new|noun}

book
bag

watch
bus
battery
shelf

## Plural patterns {new}

book - books
bag - bags
key - keys
box - boxes
watch - watches
bus - buses
battery - batteries
shelf - shelves

## These/those patterns {new}

these are
those are
these books are
those boxes are
