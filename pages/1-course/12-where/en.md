# 12. Where is the book?

## Question word {new|questionWord}

where

## Place preposition {new|preposition}

at

## Places {new|noun}

home
work
school
city
street
office

## Place word {new|fixedAdverb}

here
there

## Contracted form {new|contraction}

where's

## The patterns {new}

where is the book
where are the books
where is the manager
where are the doctors

## Practice

Where is the book?
It is on the table.
Where are the books?
They are on the shelf.

Where is the bag?
It is under the bed.
Where are the bags?
They are under the beds.

Where is the key?
It is in the box.
Where are the keys?
They are in the boxes.

Where is the manager?
He is at work.
Where are the managers?
They are at work.

Where is the doctor?
He is at the office.
Where are the doctors?
They are at the office.

Where is the worker?

Where are the workers?
They are at school.

He is at home.
Where are the sellers?
They are at home.

## Questions and answers

Where is the house?
The house is near the wall.

Where are the kitchens?
The kitchens are in the houses.

Where is the bedroom?
The bedroom is in the house.

Where are the bathrooms?
The bathrooms are in the houses.
