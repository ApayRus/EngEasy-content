# 6. I am a student. She is an engineer.

## Indefinite articles {new|article}

a
an

## Nouns {new|noun}

student
teacher
friend
boy
girl
enemy
artist
engineer
officer
expert

## Article patterns {new}

a student
a teacher
a friend
a boy
a girl
an enemy
an artist
an engineer
an officer
an expert
