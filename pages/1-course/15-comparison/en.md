# 15. This table is longer than that table.

## Adjectives {new|adjective}

long
wide
heavy
easy
hard
hot
light
beautiful
interesting

## Comparison word {new|conjunction}

than

## Short adjectives: -er / -est {new}

long | longer | the longest

hard | harder | the hardest

light | lighter | the lightest

## Spelling changes {new}

wide | wider | the widest

heavy | heavier | the heaviest

easy | easier | the easiest

hot | hotter | the hottest

## Long adjectives: more / most {new}

beautiful | more beautiful | the most beautiful

interesting | more interesting | the most interesting

## Known adjectives: three forms {new}

good | better | the best

bad | worse | the worst

big | bigger | the biggest

small | smaller | the smallest

old | older | the oldest

young | younger | the youngest

happy | happier | the happiest

busy | busier | the busiest

clean | cleaner | the cleanest

dirty | dirtier | the dirtiest
