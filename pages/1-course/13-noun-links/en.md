# 13. The doctors' photo is near the park gate.

## Nouns {new|noun}

computer
car

## Possessive pattern: one person {new}

the manager's computer
the doctor's coat
the worker's bottle
the visitor's photo

## Possessive pattern: plural people {new}

the managers' computer
the doctors' coats
the workers' bottles
the visitors' photos

## Noun + noun pattern {new}

the car door
the car window
the park bench
the park gate
